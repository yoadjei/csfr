\section{Reconstruction quality at every severity level}
\label{sec:supp:quality}

The paper tabulates three of the five severity levels. Tables~\ref{tab:supp:psnr}
and~\ref{tab:supp:ssim} give hole PSNR and hole SSIM at all five.
Table~\ref{tab:supp:hrp} gives the hallucination risk proxy (HRP), which the paper
uses as a descriptive statistic only: it flags imputed values outside the
intensity distribution of the fragment, but it cannot detect wrong values inside
it, so no value is marked as best. Table~\ref{tab:supp:cap} counts the
patch-runs at the hole-PSNR cap, the counts behind the cap-free analysis of the
paper.
